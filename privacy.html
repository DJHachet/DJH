Policy

How DJH collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use this website or get in touch.

Last updated: July 2026

Who I Am

This website is operated by DJH, an independent video and content creation business run by Samuel Coupe, based in Nottinghamshire, UK, operating as a sole trader. For any questions about this policy or your data, contact contact@djhvsuls.uk.

What Data I Collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, I collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Details about your project or enquiry, including any budget range you select

I do not collect any special category data, and I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

How I Use Your Data

I use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • Provide quotes and carry out work you request
  • Keep records of our correspondence for legitimate business purposes

The lawful basis for processing this data is your consent (given when you submit the form) and my legitimate interest in responding to and managing enquiries about my services. I will never sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing without your clear, separate consent.

How Your Data Is Handled

The contact form is powered by a third-party service, Web3Forms, which processes your submission and forwards it to my email inbox. Your message is delivered to an email address managed through Cloudflare and Google (Gmail). These providers process data on my behalf under their own privacy terms.

This website is served over a secure HTTPS connection, so information submitted through the form is encrypted in transit.

How Long I Keep It

I keep enquiry and project correspondence only for as long as necessary to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for business and legal record-keeping. If you would like your data deleted sooner, just ask.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how your data is processed
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@djhvsuls.uk and I’ll respond as quickly as I can.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Embedded content, such as YouTube videos, may set its own cookies when you interact with it — these are governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how your data is handled, please contact me first and I’ll do my best to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
