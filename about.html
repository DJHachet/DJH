About

Sam Coupe
/ DJH

Video editor, motion designer and videographer based in Nottinghamshire, UK.

Available for new projects — now

I started editing in May 2020 — completely self-taught, no film school, no shortcuts. Six years later I’ve built an audience of 899,000+ organic views and delivered over 100 projects for 50+ clients.

DJH is my independent creative studio. Every project is handled personally from concept to final delivery — no handoffs, no outsourcing. When you work with me, you work with the person actually making the thing.

I built my name producing content in some of the most competitive, editing-heavy corners of the internet — work that had to perform to survive. Product showcase videos that sold out client stock within hours. Edits that filled commission slots within 15 minutes of opening. That obsession with what actually works is what I bring to every project now.

Today my focus is automotive and commercial content — cinematic car edits, dealership social media, product and brand work. I offer both filming and full post-production: stabilised 4K capture on location across the East Midlands, or working with footage you provide from anywhere. Everything is graded, sound designed and finished in-house.

My setup is compact and discreet — no crew, no disruption. I work around your customers and your space, in and out fast, and deliver in 2–8 days after filming.

I’m open to commercial commissions and part-time creative roles, remote or hybrid, alongside ongoing freelance work.
